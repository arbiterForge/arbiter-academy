Recovery guidance

If a lab becomes blocked, stop changing the attempt. Preserve the failed attempt and the evidence already produced in your fork rather than hiding or deleting state.

If you have not started an attempt yet, begin with arbiter-academy --repository <learner-repository> prepare <lab-id>.

Diagnose before resetting

Run the authoritative check from the installed verifier and keep its result with your attempt:

arbiter-academy --repository <learner-repository> check <lab-id>

An in-checkout verifier is not authoritative. The installed verifier is the trust anchor; the selected checkout and its Git and artifact data remain untrusted inputs.

Begin a documented retry

Preserve or commit the evidence on the current attempt branch, make the learner checkout clean, and confirm that all push destinations still belong to your fork. Then run:

arbiter-academy --repository <learner-repository> reset <lab-id>

Reset archives the current attempt. Reset already prepares the next numbered attempt; do not run prepare again.
